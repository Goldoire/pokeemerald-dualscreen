# Experimental sunlight and shadows

Development builds offer **ART / SUN** in the settings page's 3D section.
ART is the default and retains the existing renderer. SUN adapts the 3DS
fork's northwest sun and face-light calculation to our native GL/GLES
meshes, with a 70% ambient floor. This is local development work, absent
from the published 0.5.0 APK.

SUN adds directional face shading, geometry-cast shading on upward terrain
faces, actor tint in shade and character silhouette shadows using the
actor's actual animation texture. Shadows clip to receiving cells and
separate stair treads; they exclude void/blocked cells and crop sprite
pixels below a raised receiving surface. Indoor maps retain ART shading.
Hull templates receive face shading once; their individual instances cast
shadows without duplicating their mesh geometry.

The caster geometry merges adjacent coplanar grid faces while preserving
holes. It ignores UV/color boundaries only for shadow tracing; visible
geometry, tree carving, roofs and texture coordinates are unchanged.
Unsupported hull geometry falls back to its original triangles.

Lighting trees and exact-position sample caches reset on connected-map or
live-map rebuilds, lighting changes, GL resource rebuilds and shutdown.
Turning SUN off rebuilds the original mesh colors, preventing cumulative
darkening. Scene admission is capped at 400,000 caster triangles, bounding
resident triangle/tree storage to approximately 22 MiB. An eight-cell
spatial index selects nearby hull casters, with a separate cap of 262,144
index nodes and 65,536 hull instances. Including its nodes, references,
metadata and sample cache, resident lighting storage is bounded to roughly
26 MiB. Temporary caster preparation needs additional memory. An
allocation failure or oversized scene retains ART, with a diagnostic log.

## Validation

Android ARMv7 debug and native Linux builds compile. Portable tests, also
run with AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer, cover camera defaults
and stability, lighting direction/reach, double-sided winding, self-shadow,
BVH capacity and gaps, stair/raised-plane shadow clipping, exact caster
merging and spatial-index coverage. Index tests cover negative bucket
coordinates, inclusive edges, hash collisions, duplicates and size limits.
Merging checks compare original and compact geometry with rays in
both directions across all three axes, including negative coordinates and
an interior hole.

The Linux integration check uses Xvfb/software GL and a disposable copy of
an existing save. It checks ART → SUN → ART in Littleroot, Rustboro and a
Pokémon Center, unchanged world geometry, no GL errors, indoor/outdoor
reset, rejected out-of-range settings and live 35-degree/120% camera options.
Screenshots, dumps, generated atlas files and the test save remain local.

```sh
python3 tools/research/run_voxel_tests.py
python3 tools/research/run_voxel_tests.py --sanitize
python3 tools/research/probe_voxel_runtime.py \
  --binary /absolute/path/to/pokeemerald \
  --save /absolute/path/to/existing.sav \
  --output /absolute/path/to/local-test-output
```

Measured scene preparation on this test host was:

| Scene | Source triangles | Caster triangles | Trees/index nodes | Before index | With index |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Littleroot and connected route | 84,160 | 58,092 | 2.7 MiB | 68–71 ms | 69–74 ms |
| Rustboro and three connected routes | 627,496 | 363,380 | 17.5 MiB | 1,030 ms | 504 ms |

These are single host samples of lighting preparation, not frame-time
benchmarks or predictions for the Thor; the memory column excludes caches
and metadata. The Rustboro result still adds a visible map-entry stall.
Device measurements and further scheduling work are necessary before
considering SUN a release default.

## Remaining limits

Terrain shadow shading is interpolated between existing vertices; it is
not a shadow map. CPU casting treats textured terrain quads as geometric
surfaces and does not sample their transparent texels. Carved hulls use
their actual geometry. Cast shadows are not applied separately to every
reused hull's vertex colors, and battle rigs retain their existing lighting.

Handheld touch layout, camera occlusion, Cut/door/live-tile changes, Surf
composition, battle entry/return and Android context restoration still
need device checks. Authored buildings/interiors, water reflections,
donor terrain relief and chunk scheduling are separate work. Their
authored-model generator passed our asset probes, but those models are
not yet loaded by this renderer or generated by the Android ROM importer.
